ML Methodology (Viva Prep)
Why Linear Regression with Lag Features?

Standard linear regression cannot naturally capture temporal ordering. To overcome this, our system engineers autoregressive lag features:

  • Lag 1: Previous day's sales to capture immediate momentum.
  • Lag 7: Same weekday of the previous week (weekly seasonality).
  • 7-Day Moving Avg: Smooths high-frequency noise to establish underlying trend.
  • Calendar Features: Day of week, month, weekend indicator.
No Data Leakage: Training and evaluation uses chronological splitting rather than random shuffling.
