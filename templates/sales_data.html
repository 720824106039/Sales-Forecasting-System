Dataset Format Requirements

The preprocessing engine validates every row. The dataset must meet the following minimum requirements:

  • * Date: Transaction date (e.g. 2025-01-15)
    Required
  • * Sales: Sales amount in numerical currency
    Required
  • Product: Product item name (used in analytics)
    Optional
  • Category: Product category / department
    Optional
  • Quantity & Price: Units sold & price per unit
    Optional
